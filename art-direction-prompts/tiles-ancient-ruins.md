# Tile Prompts — Biome 3: Ancient Lithic Ruins

**Canvas:** 8×8 px per tile (seamlessly tileable)
**Depth:** deepest biome — pre-human or non-human architecture
**Mood:** reverent dread. You are small. This is old. The artifacts came from here.
**Palette role:** deep ochre-brown fill, pale sand or faded red border

---

## Wall (carved stone block)
```
8x8 pixel art tile, ancient carved stone block, seamlessly tileable,
deep ochre-brown fill, pale sand single-pixel border,
surface detail: faint geometric relief carving or glyph fragment,
worn edges, pre-human architecture scale,
GBA retro pixel art, Caves of Qud ancient ruin aesthetic, limited 16-color palette
```

```
8x8 pixel art tile, ancient stone wall block with visible carved glyph,
```
