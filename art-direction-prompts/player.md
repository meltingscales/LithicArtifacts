# Player Sprite Prompts

**Canvas:** 8×16 px (two stacked 8×8 tiles)
**Palette:** Pyxel 16-color. Dominant: yellow (10), orange (9). Accent: 1–2 mutation colors.
**Style tags:** GBA pixel art, retro platformer, body horror, limited palette, crisp outlines

---
