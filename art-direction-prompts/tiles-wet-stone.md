# Tile Prompts — Biome 2: Wet Stone

**Canvas:** 8×8 px per tile (seamlessly tileable)
**Depth:** mid-level — natural cave beneath the biomechanical structure
**Mood:** vast and cold. Water drips. Rusting intrusions from above still appear.
**Palette role:** dark blue-gray fill, lighter blue-gray or near-white border
